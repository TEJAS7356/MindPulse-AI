How MindPulse thinks.

A plain-language explanation of the data, model, and limitations.

01

Inputs & target

The model receives self-reported lifestyle features such as sleep, stress, mood, activity, screen time, social interaction, and satisfaction. It predicts one of three target categories: Low Risk, Moderate Risk, or High Risk.

02

Preprocessing

Numeric missing values are imputed with a median. Physical activity is one-hot encoded. The data is split into training and held-out testing data.

03

Random Forest

A collection of decision trees learns patterns from the educational dataset. The saved model returns a category and class probabilities.

04

Explainability

Global feature importance and directional explanations show which inputs the model used most. These are model behaviors, not medical causes.

05

Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and educational, not a validated clinical population. Facial-expression analysis describes visible expression only. This application cannot diagnose or replace professional mental-health advice.
