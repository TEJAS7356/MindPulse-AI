SUPPORT IN INDIA

Find someone to talk to.

You do not need to take an assessment or get a particular result to use these support options.

NEARBY CARE

Find local support

For in-person help, contact or visit a nearby government district hospital, Community Health Centre (CHC), Primary Health Centre (PHC), or Ayushman Arogya Mandir. Ask for mental-health services or the District Mental Health Programme (DMHP). What is available can vary by district.

Government of India: district and community mental-health services ↗

MindPulse does not verify individual facilities or make appointments. Confirm local service details with your state/district health department or the facility before travelling.

MENTAL-HEALTH SUPPORT

Talk to someone now

Tele-MANAS is the Government of India’s national tele-mental-health service. If you are unsure where to find local care, ask them about available in-person support and referrals.

Call 14416 Call 1-800-891-4416

If you are unsure what to say, you can start with: “I’m having a hard time and would like to talk to someone.”

Official Tele-MANAS website ↗

IMMEDIATE DANGER

Urgent help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call India’s emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a MindPulse result.

Call 112

112 is India’s unified emergency response number. MindPulse is not an emergency service and does not monitor or contact responders.
