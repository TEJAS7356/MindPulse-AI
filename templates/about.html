THE IDEA BEHIND MINDPULSE

Technology with a softer edge.

MindPulse AI is a college AIML project exploring how machine learning, computer vision, and generative AI can support personal reflection.

What it does

The platform estimates wellbeing risk from self-reported lifestyle information, explains the model’s broad feature use, offers what-if simulations, visual-expression analysis, and a supportive AI companion.

What it does not do

It does not diagnose depression, anxiety disorders, mental illness, or any medical condition. It does not replace a qualified professional.

Data & privacy

Accounts and records are isolated by user ID in SQLite. For a production deployment, Render’s ephemeral filesystem means SQLite is not suitable for permanent storage.

Safety disclaimer
This application is an educational AI/ML project that provides a wellbeing risk estimate based on self-reported lifestyle information and optional facial-expression analysis. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and should not be used as a substitute for professional mental-health advice.
